01 · Ordering

02 · Shipping

Standard dispatch uses priority overnight on a Mon–Thu schedule, which covers most domestic addresses in 1–2 business days. Same-day courier within metro Chicago and saturday cold-chain delivery are available on request — email us before ordering for a quote.
If a courier flags a delay that pushes the package outside the 48-hour cold-pack window, you'll get a direct email from our operations lead — not an automated alert. We'll lay out the replacement-or-refund options before the package arrives, not after.
We ship to research institutions in Canada, the UK, and most of the EU. Customs documentation is prepared on our side, but the receiving institution is responsible for clearance and any local duties. We do not ship to private residences outside the US.

04 · COAs & documentation

Each product detail page has a Documentation tab linking the lot-specific COA, HPLC chromatogram, and mass spectrum. The lot number on your vial maps directly to the document on file. The dispatch email also includes a deep link to the matching COA.
Lot-specific. Every COA references one lot's actual HPLC trace, integrated purity value, observed mass, and synthesis date. We do not publish averaged or representative documents. If your lot number is on the vial and not on the document, it's not the right COA.
Yes. We retain lot records indefinitely. Email orders@pepcision.com with the lot number printed on your vial and we'll send the archived COA the same business day.

05 · Returns & issues

Because these are temperature-sensitive research materials, we cannot accept returns once a shipment has been opened or the cold chain has been broken on the buyer's end. We do replace or refund any lot that arrives compromised, mislabeled, or out of spec — see the next item.
If a shipment arrives outside the 48-hour cold window, the ice packs are fully thawed, the vial is damaged, or the lot fails to match the documented spec, photograph the package on receipt and email us within 24 hours. We dispatch a replacement same-day where stock allows, or refund in full.
Email us before the dispatch confirmation goes out and we can swap the line item or cancel the order. Once an order has shipped, we can't accept a return on a temperature-sensitive product, but we'll work with you on a discount for the replacement order if the error is on the ordering side.
