Documentation & specifications

BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound-157) is a pentadecapeptide derived from a sequence isolated from human gastric juice. It is supplied here as a lyophilized powder for laboratory research applications, including in vitro studies of tissue recovery, angiogenesis, and inflammatory signaling pathways.

Each vial is sealed under inert atmosphere and accompanied by lot-specific documentation. Reconstitution and storage guidance is provided on the Storage tab.

  • FormatLyophilized powder
  • Unit size5 mg per vial
  • Purity99.1% by reverse-phase HPLC
  • SequenceGEPPPGKPADDAGLV
  • Theoretical mass1419.5 Da (monoisotopic)
  • Observed mass1419.5 Da (ESI-MS)
  • Storage (lyophilized)−20 °C, light-protected

Lyophilized: −20 °C in original sealed vial. Stable for 24 months from synthesis date when stored as directed. Avoid repeated thaw-freeze of sealed vials at room temperature.

Reconstituted: Dissolve in bacteriostatic water or research-grade saline. Use within 7 days when stored at 2–8 °C. For longer storage, aliquot and freeze at −20 °C; avoid more than 2–3 freeze-thaw cycles.

Shipping: Insulated packaging with two ice packs, 48-hr cold window. If the package arrives outside this window, document on receipt and contact us within 24 hours.
