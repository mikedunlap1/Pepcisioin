A short guide to how Pepcision presents products and documentation.

Research use only

Products listed on this site are offered for laboratory research use only. They are not intended for human or animal consumption, clinical use, diagnosis, treatment, cure, or prevention of disease. Pepcision does not provide dosing, reconstitution, or medical guidance.

Understanding a COA

A certificate of analysis is lot-specific documentation from an analytical laboratory. When a COA is available, match its lot number to the lot shown on the product packaging. Review the stated test method, purity result, observed mass, and report date.

Domestic fulfillment

Pepcision is a USA-based company. Orders are fulfilled from Tampa, Florida. Some research compounds may be sourced internationally before domestic fulfillment; the shipping point for customer orders is Tampa, not a direct overseas shipment.

Product information

Names, quantities, and analytical details are provided for identification and research context. Confirm current availability and documentation with us before placing an order.
