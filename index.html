02Methods

What goes into a Pepcision lot before it ships.

A four-step process from synthesis to dispatch. We document each step; the lot number on the vial maps to the record in our QC log.

  1. Solid-phase synthesis

    Each peptide is synthesized via Fmoc-based SPPS at an audited contract facility. Synthesis records — resin, scale, coupling reagents — are filed against the lot.

  2. Reverse-phase HPLC purification & analysis

    Crude product is purified by preparative HPLC and re-analyzed on an analytical column. We publish the chromatogram and integrated purity for the exact lot in your shipment.

  3. Mass-spec confirmation

    ESI or MALDI mass spectrometry confirms the observed monoisotopic mass against theoretical. Both values appear on the COA. Mismatch = lot is rejected.

  4. Lyophilization, packaging, cold dispatch

    Lyophilized into amber vials under sterile conditions, packed with two ice packs and an insulated liner, and routed via priority overnight. Dispatch window: Mon–Thu only.
