How we operate

Documentation as the product.

Most suppliers will sell you a vial. Few will let you trace a lot number back to the chromatogram. That traceability is what we sell.

Research-grade sourcing

Every line follows controlled sourcing and standardized intake review at an ISO-audited contract facility.

Lot-linked records

The lot number on your vial maps directly to its HPLC chromatogram, mass spectrum, and COA on file.

Cold chain to your door

Two ice packs, insulated liner, and a temperature label. If the label trips, we replace the lot.

Verified researchers only

First orders go through a quick institutional or independent-researcher check. We don't sell to anyone we can't verify.

Research use policy

Strict research-use-only.

All Pepcision products are sold strictly for laboratory research use only. They are not intended for human or animal consumption, and are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

Buyers are responsible for lawful acquisition, appropriate storage, safe handling, and correct use in legitimate research environments.
