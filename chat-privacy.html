Chat demo privacy

This temporary demo lets the team try the Pepcision chatbot on desktop and mobile. It uses preset approved answers, with no live AI, order access, or private logging service.

Your messages stay in this page

Questions and replies are processed in your browser. This demo does not send chat messages to Pepcision, OpenAI, Airtable, or a chat server. It does not save conversations in cookies or browser storage. Reload the page or choose Clear chat to clear the displayed conversation.

GitHub Pages serves the website and may process normal website request metadata. Please do not enter personal, payment, account, or medical details.

Demo limits

Try the suggested topics for shipping, documentation, availability, and support. Unrecognized questions receive a support message. Medical, dosing, preparation, and human or animal use requests receive a refusal. The demo cannot create support tickets or verify inventory or lab results.
