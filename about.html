About Pepcision · Est. 2024

We sell what we'd order ourselves.

Pepcision was started by a small team of researchers and operators tired of opaque suppliers, vague COAs, and shipments that arrived warm. The catalog is small on purpose. Each lot is documented down to the chromatogram.

Position

A research peptide supplier that treats documentation as the product, not the packaging.

Most research-peptide suppliers will sell you a vial. Most will even attach a COA. Few will let you trace a lot number from your hand back through the analytical chromatogram and into the synthesis record. That traceability — and the operational discipline it requires — is what we sell. Peptides are the byproduct.

01How we operate

01 / 04

The catalog

Forty-something SKUs across healing, secretagogue, mitochondrial, copper, and neurochemistry lines. We don't add a peptide until we can synthesize it cleanly enough to hit our 98% HPLC floor and source it from a facility we've audited. We'd rather have a short catalog with documented lots than a long one full of question marks.

02 / 04

Synthesis & QC

SPPS is run at an ISO-audited contract facility. Crude product is HPLC-purified, then re-analyzed on an analytical column. Mass-spec confirms the observed monoisotopic mass against theoretical. If they don't match within tolerance, the lot is rejected — not relabeled, not sold at a discount.

03 / 04

Cold chain & dispatch

Mon–Thu dispatch only, because Friday shipments sit in warehouses over the weekend and cold packs don't last that long. Each box ships with two ice packs, an insulated liner, and a temperature label. If the label trips, we replace the lot.

04 / 04

Sales discipline

We don't sell to anyone we can't verify. First orders go through a quick institutional or independent-researcher check. We're slower than the wild-west suppliers and we're fine with that — it's the cost of operating cleanly.

02The team

Small, technical, accessible.

Three people run Pepcision. There is no sales department. Questions about a lot, a chromatogram, or an upcoming order go to one of the three of us directly.
